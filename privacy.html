Privacy Policy

Trivia Pipeline · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Trivia Pipeline ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Tricky Trivia and Tough Trivia YouTube channels to upload his own videos to his own channels and to view the statistics of his own channels. This policy explains what the app does with data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and set thumbnails on the signed-in owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly: to read the statistics of the owner's own channel and videos (for example views, watch time and audience retention), so he can see which videos work best.
  • The app never accesses other users' accounts or data, and does not read comments or any personal data about viewers. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated by YouTube and does not identify individual viewers.

How data is stored

  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. It is never sent anywhere except to Google's servers when uploading.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails are the channel owner's own content.
  • Statistics are shown only to the owner in his own terminal and are not stored by the app. If the owner ever keeps a copy, it stays on his own computer and is deleted within 7 days if access is revoked.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from the computer.

Data deletion

The app does not store YouTube data. If access is revoked, or on request to the contact address below, the local token file and any local copies of statistics are deleted within 7 days.
