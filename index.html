Trivia Pipeline

A private publishing tool for the Tricky Trivia and Tough Trivia YouTube channels.

Trivia Pipeline is a small script that the channel owner runs on his own computer to upload finished quiz videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to his own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: it has one user (the channel owner), it does not access anyone else's account, and it only uses permission to upload videos.
